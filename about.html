Engineering Story

I am drawn to work where the answer cannot live in one tool or one discipline. Requirements matter, but so do flight behavior, human judgment, manufacturability, and clear communication.

That mindset has shown up in avionics work, published research, UAV projects, and NASA L'SPACE proposal work. Each project has pushed me to understand the full system, not just the part in front of me.

Flight training keeps the work grounded. It reminds me that good engineering has to make sense when people are operating the system, not only when the model looks clean.
